iPhone and iPad

An iPhone can't run programs from a USB stick: Apple only allows apps from the App Store. But it can still use almost everything in the kit, in three ways. Set it up now, while you have internet.

Three ways to use it

1. Connect to a computer running the kitEverything works in Safari: AI with sources, library, maps, cards. Needs a computer with power. How
2. Apps on the iPhone, files from the stickLibrary, AI and maps work on the phone alone, even with the computer off. Set it up beforehand. How
3. Open files straight from the stickPDFs (Survival-Data, your documents) open in the Files app. No AI, library search or maps this way.

Install the apps (free, needs internet)

KiwixOffline library: reads the .zim books from the stick's library folder.
PocketPal AIThe AI on the phone: loads the .gguf models from the stick's models folder. Free to download, and loading a model and chatting offline is free. It also sells extras (PocketPal Plus, add-on packs): you don't need any of them for the kit. Alternative: LLM Farm (free, open-source, loads the same files).
Organic MapsOffline maps with GPS and directions. It uses its own maps, not the kit's: download your country or state inside the app.

Search for each name in the App Store. Open each app once while online, in case it asks to download something. If an app shows a subscription screen, close it: everything on this page works without paying. PocketPal is rated 18+ in the App Store because of optional chat characters, so for a child's phone LLM Farm is the better choice.

Connect the stick

  • iPhone 15 or newer, and iPads with USB-C: plug in a USB-C stick, or a USB-A stick with a small adapter.
  • Older iPhones (Lightning): use Apple's Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter. If the stick doesn't show up, plug a charger into the adapter's power port: many sticks need more power than the phone gives.
  • Open the Files app → Browse → the stick appears under Locations (often named SURVIVAL).

The stick must be exFAT (the builder formats it that way). iPhones can't read NTFS sticks.

Library on the iPhone (Kiwix)

  1. Check free space: Settings → General → iPhone Storage. The books are big, so pick the ones that fit.
  2. In Files, open the stick → library. Each .zim file is one book.
  3. Touch and hold a book → Copy, go to On My iPhone → Kiwix, then touch and hold → Paste. Or open Kiwix and use its button to open or import a file, then pick the book on the stick.
  4. The book appears in Kiwix's library. Search and read offline.

Good small books to start with: zimgit-medicine, zimgit-water, zimgit-post-disaster, zimgit-food-preparation, zimgit-knots (all English, under 1 GB together), wikipedia_pt_medicine (Portuguese, about 270 MB). Wikipedia medicine in English is about 1.7 GB.

Problems?

    

      
The stick doesn't appear in FilesUnplug and replug. With a Lightning adapter, connect a charger to it. The stick must be exFAT.

      
"Not enough storage"Copy fewer or smaller books, or delete photos and apps you don't need. You can also read a book straight from the stick in Kiwix without copying it.

      
The AI app closes when loading the modelThe phone doesn't have enough memory: use Llama-3.2-1B and close other apps.

      
Safari can't open the kit's addressCheck the computer is on the iPhone's hotspot, type http:// and :8080, and on Windows click Allow if the firewall asked.

Problemas?

    

      
O pendrive não aparece no ArquivosTire e coloque de novo. Com adaptador Lightning, ligue um carregador nele. O pendrive precisa ser exFAT.

      
"Armazenamento insuficiente"Copie menos livros ou livros menores, ou apague fotos e apps que não precisa. Também dá para ler um livro direto do pendrive no Kiwix, sem copiar.

      
O app da IA fecha ao carregar o modeloFalta memória no celular: use o Llama-3.2-1B e feche outros apps.

      
O Safari não abre o endereço do kitConfira se o computador está no Acesso Pessoal do iPhone, digite http:// e :8080, e no Windows clique em Permitir se o firewall perguntou.
