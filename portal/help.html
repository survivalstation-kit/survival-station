Help: how this works

Survival Station is an offline emergency kit on a USB drive. It needs no internet and installs nothing on the computer. Everything below runs from the drive.

In a life-threatening emergency, don't read this page. Go straight to the Emergency cards, and call for help if any phone network works (112 / 911).

What's inside

⛑️ Emergency cardsShort first-aid and disaster steps: CPR, bleeding, burns, water, flood, fire, nuclear and chemical emergencies, signals, radio. They work even if nothing else does, and they print well.
💬 Ask the AIA chat assistant that runs on this computer. Describe your situation and get step-by-step advice. It looks up the offline library first and shows its sources.
📚 Offline libraryMedical encyclopedias, water and food guides, repair manuals and Wikipedia, all searchable. Which books you have depends on how the drive was built.
🗺️ Offline mapA world map plus detailed street maps of the regions that were downloaded. Tap a spot to get coordinates for rescuers, or pin water, shelter, meeting points and dangers for everyone connected to the kit.
⛅ WeatherForecast for 7 days and official alerts (USA), whenever this device still has some internet, such as a phone's mobile data. The last forecast stays saved on the device, with its date, for when the connection is gone. It also says how to follow the weather without internet.
👪 Family planContacts, meeting places and each person's blood type, allergies and medicines. Print it, and on the kit computer save a copy on the stick, locked with a password.
✅ ChecklistsGo-bag, supplies at home, documents, and before you evacuate. Tick what is ready, add your own items, and print them. Saved on the device and, on the kit computer, on the stick.
📦 SuppliesYour stored water, food, medicines and batteries with expiry dates: what has expired turns red, what expires within 30 days orange, and the home page warns you. Tips on rotating stock.
🫂 Psychological first aidHow to support someone in shock or grief (look, listen, link), what to say and not say, calming techniques, children, normal reactions, looking after yourself, and help lines. Based on the WHO guide.
🗣️ PhrasebookPicture cards to point and show in English, Portuguese and Spanish: urgent needs, health (with a 0–10 pain scale), needs, people, answers. Tap one to show it full screen, and 🔊 to have the device say it.
🖨️ Print packThe emergency cards, radio frequencies, a Morse code chart, the family plan and start instructions, ready to print or save as PDF.
🔒 Private documentsIDs, insurance papers and other personal files go in the kit's private folder, which phones can never see. Lock them with PRIVATE-DOCS.bat or on the My kit page (Mac/Linux: bash launch/private-docs.sh). Without the password they can't be opened, so write it down somewhere safe.
🛠️ ToolsCalculators and signals that need no AI: bleach drops to make water safe, water and food for your group, a CPR metronome that beats the 100–120 compressions a minute and counts the rounds, SOS by screen flash, whistle tone or Morse code, a power calculator (power banks, solar, how long a laptop runs the kit, and generator safety), a full Morse code guide (alphabet chart, timing, how to send with a torch, whistle, mirror or by tapping when trapped, key signals, translators from text to dots and dashes and back, and ear practice), sunrise, sunset and the sun's direction, and a compass where the phone allows it.
🔎 SearchOne search box for the whole kit (also on the home page): emergency cards, radio and help pages, the Survival Data guides (titles and the text inside the PDFs, opening at the right page), your documents, and the full text of every library book. Scanned PDFs (pictures of pages) can't be searched.
📡 RadioEmergency frequencies, how to call for help, and how to set up a Baofeng UV-5R or TIDRADIO TD-H3 PLUS, with a channel file for CHIRP, and Meshtastic: cheap LoRa devices to text and share positions with no phone network. Print it and keep it with the radios.
📖 BooksThe Art of War by Sun Tzu in English and Portuguese (a new translation from the Chinese, free to copy), read aloud by the device with 🔊 Listen, official US guides on nuclear, biological and chemical emergencies (PDF), and a reading list with links to borrow or buy other books, and to free classics.
🕶️ Private browsingHow to use Tor Browser and Proton VPN Free, when some internet is left: browse without showing where you are, reach blocked sites, bridges and the pluggable transports (obfs4, WebTunnel, Snowflake, meek) when Tor is blocked, phones, and safe habits.
🔐 Private favoritesAddresses to keep secret (for example sites to open in Tor Browser), encrypted with your password. On the kit computer they are also kept on the stick, encrypted. Copy an address and paste it into Tor Browser. A forgotten password can't be recovered.
📻 Survival DataA collection of survival, radio and medical manuals (PDFs, in English).
📄 Your documentsFiles the owner put on the drive: family contacts, medication lists, local maps.
🧰 My kitWhat is on this kit, a Check the kit test that starts each part and sees that it answers, and buttons to add more, update or repair it.
⚡ Low powerThe Low power switch on the home page: dark pages, shorter answers, and the AI answers without looking up the library. On battery, the kit suggests it, and the start window picks the smallest AI model.

Use the language menu at the top of any page to switch between English and Português. Your choice is remembered on that device.

Starting it

  1. Plug the USB drive into a computer and open it in the file explorer.
  2. Double-click the start file for that computer:
    • Windows: START-WINDOWS.bat. If Windows warns "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
    • Mac: START-MAC.command. If it's blocked, right-click it and choose Open.
    • Linux / Raspberry Pi: open a terminal on the drive and type bash START-LINUX.sh.
  3. A black window opens. If it asks you to choose an AI model, wait 10 seconds for the default, or type the number of a smaller one on an old or slow computer.
  4. After a short wait, the browser opens this portal at http://localhost:8080.
  5. Keep the black window open while you use the kit. Closing it (or pressing a key in it) shuts everything down.

If the AI can't start, the emergency cards, this help page and your documents still open as plain files: just open portal/index.html on the drive. If the kit isn't running, its home page shows the kit's folder and which start file to double-click, and switches to the full kit by itself once the AI starts.

Emergency cards

  • Open them from Emergency at the top of any page. Use the buttons at the top to jump to a topic.
  • They are plain pages, so they work without the AI and even without the start program.
  • Print them now (Ctrl+P / ⌘P) and keep the paper copy with the drive.

Ask the AI

  1. Open Ask AI. Type what is happening in plain words, e.g. "My neighbour cut his hand deeply on glass, it won't stop bleeding", and press Enter. Or tap one of the example questions.
  2. While it works you'll see Searching the offline library… → Reading articles… → Thinking…, then the answer appears word by word.
  3. Under the answer, From the offline library lists the articles it used. Numbers like [1] in the answer point to them. Tap one to read the full article.
  4. Ask follow-up questions in the same chat. New starts a fresh conversation.
  5. Stop (the Send button while it's writing) cuts an answer short.
  • Too slow? Untick Look it up in the library. Answers come faster but without sources. Or restart and choose a smaller model.
  • It can be wrong. It's a small AI running offline. For anything medical, check the emergency cards and the source articles. It never replaces a doctor.
  • Conversations are saved only in this browser on this device.

Photos 📷

Start the kit with the model marked [photos / fotos] (Gemma 3 4B). A 📷 button then appears next to the question box: take or pick a photo, add a question ("What is this plant?", "Is this wound infected?", "What does this medicine label say?") and press Send. It takes 20–60 seconds. Photo answers can be wrong: never eat a wild plant or mushroom because of one, and treat every snake bite as an emergency.

Voice 🎤 🔊

  • Hear the answers: tap 🔊 Listen under an answer, or tick Read answers aloud. It uses the device's own voices, on every computer and phone.
  • Speak your question on the computer running the kit (Windows or Linux): tap 🎤, talk, then tap ⏹. The kit turns it into text and sends it.
  • On a phone: phones may only use the microphone on the kit's secure address, the same address with https and port 8443 (e.g. https://192.168.43.20:8443/; the start window and the home page show it). The first time, the phone warns that the connection is "not private": that's expected, because the kit uses its own certificate. Choose Advanced / Show details and continue. Then 🎤 works as on the computer. On the computer, click Allow if the firewall asks about Caddy.
  • Or dictate with the keyboard: tap the microphone on the phone's keyboard. On most phones it works offline; turn on offline speech in the keyboard settings ahead of time.

Offline library

  • Open Offline library on the Home page. It opens in a new tab, filtered to your language.
  • Click a book's cover to open it. Inside a book, the search box at the top searches that book.
  • To search every book at once, use the search on the library's front page, or the 🔎 Search the library link under any AI answer.
  • English and Portuguese books are searched separately. Use the language filter on the library page to switch.

Offline map

  • Move: drag with the mouse or one finger. Zoom: mouse wheel, pinch, or the + / − buttons.
  • Coordinates: tap or click any spot. A pin appears, and the bottom bar shows latitude and longitude (e.g. -22.94642, -43.20000) in two formats. Read them out to rescuers over phone or radio, or press Copy.
  • Street-level detail exists only for the regions that were downloaded. Elsewhere you'll see the world overview.
  • Find a place: type in the box at the top left: a town, neighbourhood, mountain or lake, worldwide for towns and in full detail for the countries with a street map on the kit. Type hospital or airport to list the nearest ones to where the map is looking.
  • The ◎ locate me button only appears on the computer running the kit. Phones connected over Wi-Fi can't use GPS here; use Organic Maps on the phone instead.
  • Shared pins: tap a spot, then 📍 Pin it for everyone: choose water, shelter, meeting point, danger, medical help, food, power or other, add a name and a note. Everyone connected to the kit (computer and phones) sees the pins within seconds; tap a pin for its details or to remove it. Sharing needs the kit running on Windows (Windows may ask to allow "Windows PowerShell" through the firewall: click Allow); elsewhere pins stay on that device.
  • The map needs the start program running. It won't work if you open the page as a file.

Using it on phones

Phones can't run programs from a USB stick, so the computer does the work and phones connect to it like a website:

  1. Turn on the phone's Hotspot / Personal Hotspot. It works with no signal and no data.
  2. Connect the computer to that hotspot's Wi-Fi. Or put the computer and phones on any shared Wi-Fi router.
  3. Start the kit on the computer. The black window lists addresses like http://192.168.43.20:8080/, and the home page shows a QR code: point the phone's camera at it to open the kit, with nothing to type. A phone that's already connected shows the same code on its home page, to pass on to the next phone.
  4. On each phone, open the browser and type that address exactly, including :8080.

Several phones can use it at once. If there are several addresses, try the one starting with 192.168. On Android, you can also install the apps in apps/android/ (Kiwix, PocketPal AI, Organic Maps, Meshtastic) ahead of time to use the library, AI and maps without a computer.

iPhone or iPad? See the iPhone and iPad setup: hotspot, apps, copying books and AI models from the stick, and saving the cards as a PDF.

Your documents

The Home page lists files saved in portal/docs/ on the drive. Click one to open it. To add files, copy them into that folder, then (with the setup computer) run setup\build-drive.ps1 -Drive X: -IndexOnly so they appear in the list. You can always open them straight from the folder too.

My kit

  • My kit lists what is on this kit: AI models, library books, maps and extras, with their sizes and the free space.
  • Check the kit → ▶ Test everything starts each part (AI, library, maps, voice, search) and shows which ones really answer. Do it after building or copying a kit, while you still have time to fix it.
  • On the computer running the kit: Add more, Check for updates and Verify / repair open the builder window, and the private documents can be locked and opened.

Problems?

The black window says the AI did not startClose it and start again, choosing a smaller model. Old computers with less than 8 GB of memory need the smallest one. Close other programs to free memory.
"AI: offline" on the Home pageThe start program isn't running, or it was closed. Start it again and keep the black window open.
Answers are very slowNormal on old computers. Untick Look it up in the library, ask shorter questions, or restart with a smaller model. A USB 3 port (often blue) loads faster.
The phone can't open the addressCheck that the phone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. Type the address exactly, including http:// and :8080. On Windows, allow access when the firewall asks (or allow "llama-server" and "kiwix-serve" in Windows Defender Firewall). Try another address from the list.
"Library: offline"Right after starting, the library needs a few seconds to open its books from the stick: the home page switches to "running" by itself (or press F5). If it stays offline, the drive has no library files or the library server didn't start: restart the kit.
The map is empty or shows a messageOpen it through the start program (http://localhost:8080/map.html), not as a file. If it says there are no maps, the drive was built without them.
Mac: "cannot be opened"Right-click START-MAC.command → Open → Open. Or in Terminal: bash /Volumes/<drive>/START-MAC.command.
Linux: permission deniedRun it with bash START-LINUX.sh (not ./). The launcher copies the programs to /tmp if the drive won't run them.
Wrong languageUse the language menu at the top right of any page.
"Could not reach the kit helper"The kit was updated while it was running, or its start window was closed. Shut it down with a key in the black window and start it again.
Servers still running after closing the browserThat's on purpose: phones may still be using the kit. Use the red Shut down the kit button on the home page when you're done.

Shutting down and saving power

  • Closing the browser does NOT stop the kit: the AI, library and maps keep running (phones may be using them). To stop it, click the red ⏻ Shut down the kit button on the home page (on the computer running the kit), or click the black window and press any key, or close that window (Windows). On Mac / Linux, press Ctrl+C in its window. Then you can remove the drive safely.
  • Turn on ⚡ Low power on the home page to make the battery last longer.
  • On battery, the AI uses a lot of power while it's writing an answer. Shut the kit down when you don't need it, and lower the screen brightness.
  • Keep a spare copy of the drive and a printed set of emergency cards somewhere else.

Starting a computer from the stick (if its disk fails)

A kit made bootable with MAKE-BOOTABLE.bat (Windows) can start almost any PC, even one whose own disk or Windows no longer works, into Linux Mint, a free system that runs from the stick. From there you can use the whole kit and copy your files from the computer's disk to safety. Nothing is installed or changed on the computer.

Make the stick bootable (once)

  1. It erases the stick. If it already has a kit, first copy the kit to the computer with COPY-KIT.bat → This computer.
  2. Double-click MAKE-BOOTABLE.bat, allow administrator rights, type the stick's letter, then type ERASE. It installs Ventoy (free, open source) and downloads Linux Mint XFCE (~2.9 GB), checking both against their official checksums.
  3. Copy the kit back onto the stick with COPY-KIT.bat (or build it with MAKE-USB.bat). The kit works on Windows, Mac and Linux exactly as before. Run MAKE-BOOTABLE.bat again later to update Linux Mint; once the stick is bootable, it erases nothing.

Start a computer from it

  1. Plug in the stick, turn the computer on and keep pressing its boot-menu key: usually F12, F11, F9, F8 or Esc (it often shows briefly on screen). Choose the USB stick.
  2. In the Ventoy menu choose linuxmint…, then Boot in normal mode. The first time on a computer with Secure Boot, Ventoy asks to enroll its key: follow its screens once.
  3. Linux Mint starts in a minute or two. Open the file manager, open the SURVIVAL drive, right-click in the folder → Open in Terminal, and type bash START-LINUX.sh. The kit opens in Firefox.
  4. Rescue your files: the computer's own disk appears in the file manager (if it still works); copy what matters to the stick or another drive.

Works on most PCs and Intel Macs (hold Option at start-up). Apple computers with M1 or newer chips can't start Linux from a stick this way. Changes made inside Linux Mint (settings, Wi-Fi passwords) are forgotten when it shuts down; files you save onto the SURVIVAL drive are kept.

Sharing the kit (no internet needed)

Give a copy to family, neighbours or a shelter. On a Windows computer, double-click COPY-KIT.bat on the drive.

  1. Copy from: this kit is picked for you.
  2. Copy to: the other USB drive, or This computer to keep a copy on the computer (it gets a desktop shortcut).
  3. Check the green Fits line, then click Copy. If the other drive is FAT32, click Format as exFAT first (this erases it).

If the copy stops, click Copy again: files already copied are skipped. On a Mac or Linux computer, copy every file and folder from one drive to the other with the file manager.

Updating the drive (needs internet)

Easiest: on the Windows computer running the kit, open My kit and click Add more, Check for updates or Verify / repair. Or double-click MAKE-USB.bat on the drive, choose the drive, and click Check for updates. It lists newer library editions, a newer AI engine, maps over a year old and a newer Survival-Data, with their sizes, and updates them if you say yes. In the same window you can tick more books, maps or languages and click Build the drive. With internet, the kit's own pages and tools come straight from the newest release on GitHub. The home page's 🔄 Check for updates button tells you when a new version is out. If the kit was running during an update, shut it down and start it again to use the new version. Full instructions are in README.md (English) and LEIA-ME.md (Português) on the drive.

About this kit

Survival Station is a reference tool, not a substitute for emergency services, doctors or official instructions. The AI can be wrong, and the library and guides may be out of date or not fit your situation. Call for professional help whenever you can, follow the authorities' instructions, and check anything medical against the emergency cards and a qualified person. Use radio transmitters only as your country's law allows.

Use at your own risk. Survival Station brings together free material published by others (the library, maps and AI models) with short summaries of public emergency guidance (the cards, radio page and tools). None of it has been reviewed by medical, legal or emergency professionals for this kit. It is free and open source under the MIT licence, provided "as is", without warranty of any kind; its authors and contributors accept no responsibility or liability for errors, misinformation, misuse, its answers or the material it contains. The programs, AI models, books, maps and data on the kit keep their own licences, listed in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md at the top of the drive (with LICENSE). If you share the kit, they go with it.

Como iniciar

  1. Conecte o pendrive no computador e abra-o no explorador de arquivos.
  2. Clique duas vezes no arquivo de início do seu sistema:
    • Windows: START-WINDOWS.bat. Se aparecer "O Windows protegeu o computador", clique em Mais informações → Executar assim mesmo.
    • Mac: START-MAC.command. Se for bloqueado, clique com o botão direito e escolha Abrir.
    • Linux / Raspberry Pi: abra um terminal no pendrive e digite bash START-LINUX.sh.
  3. Uma janela preta abre. Se ela pedir para escolher um modelo de IA, espere 10 segundos para usar o padrão, ou digite o número de um modelo menor se o computador for antigo ou lento.
  4. Depois de alguns instantes, o navegador abre este portal em http://localhost:8080.
  5. Deixe a janela preta aberta enquanto usa o kit. Fechar a janela (ou apertar uma tecla nela) desliga tudo.

Se a IA não iniciar, os cartões de emergência, esta ajuda e seus documentos ainda abrem como arquivos comuns: abra portal/index.html no pendrive. Se o kit não estiver rodando, a página inicial mostra a pasta do kit e qual arquivo de início clicar, e passa sozinha para o kit completo quando a IA iniciar.

Cartões de emergência

  • Abra em Emergência, no topo de qualquer página. Use os botões no início para pular para um assunto.
  • São páginas simples: funcionam sem a IA e até sem o programa de início.
  • Imprima agora (Ctrl+P) e guarde a cópia em papel junto com o pendrive.

Perguntar à IA

  1. Abra Perguntar à IA. Escreva o que está acontecendo com suas palavras, por exemplo "Meu vizinho cortou a mão no vidro e não para de sangrar", e aperte Enter. Ou toque numa das perguntas de exemplo.
  2. Enquanto trabalha, aparece Buscando na biblioteca… → Lendo artigos… → Pensando…, e depois a resposta surge palavra por palavra.
  3. Embaixo da resposta, Da biblioteca offline mostra os artigos usados. Números como [1] na resposta apontam para eles. Toque num artigo para ler tudo.
  4. Faça perguntas de continuação na mesma conversa. Nova começa outra conversa.
  5. Parar (o botão Enviar enquanto ela escreve) interrompe a resposta.
  • Muito lenta? Desmarque Consultar a biblioteca. A resposta sai mais rápido, mas sem fontes. Ou reinicie e escolha um modelo menor.
  • A IA pode errar. É uma IA pequena, rodando offline. Em assuntos médicos, confira os cartões de emergência e os artigos de origem. Não substitui um médico.
  • As conversas ficam salvas só neste navegador, neste aparelho.

Fotos 📷

Inicie o kit com o modelo marcado [photos / fotos] (Gemma 3 4B). Aparece um botão 📷 ao lado da caixa de pergunta: tire ou escolha uma foto, escreva uma pergunta ("Que planta é esta?", "Este ferimento está infeccionado?", "O que diz esta bula?") e aperte Enviar. Leva de 20 a 60 segundos. Respostas sobre fotos podem estar erradas: nunca coma uma planta ou cogumelo do mato por causa de uma, e trate qualquer picada de cobra como emergência.

Voz 🎤 🔊

  • Ouvir as respostas: toque em 🔊 Ouvir embaixo da resposta, ou marque Ler as respostas em voz alta. Usa as vozes do próprio aparelho, em qualquer computador ou celular.
  • Falar a pergunta no computador que roda o kit (Windows ou Linux): toque em 🎤, fale e toque em ⏹. O kit transforma em texto e envia.
  • No celular: o celular só pode usar o microfone no endereço seguro do kit, o mesmo endereço com https e porta 8443 (ex.: https://192.168.43.20:8443/; a janela de início e a página inicial mostram). Na primeira vez o celular avisa que a conexão "não é particular": é normal, porque o kit usa um certificado próprio. Escolha Avançado / Mostrar detalhes e continue. Aí o 🎤 funciona como no computador. No computador, clique em Permitir se o firewall perguntar sobre o Caddy.
  • Ou dite pelo teclado: toque no microfone do teclado do celular. Na maioria dos celulares funciona offline; ative o reconhecimento de voz offline nas configurações do teclado antes.

Biblioteca offline

  • Abra Biblioteca offline na página Início. Ela abre numa nova aba, já filtrada no seu idioma.
  • Clique na capa de um livro para abrir. Dentro do livro, a caixa de busca no topo procura naquele livro.
  • Para buscar em todos os livros, use a busca da página inicial da biblioteca, ou o link 🔎 Buscar isto na biblioteca embaixo de qualquer resposta da IA.

Mapa offline

  • Mover: arraste com o mouse ou um dedo. Zoom: rodinha do mouse, movimento de pinça, ou os botões + / −.
  • Coordenadas: toque ou clique em qualquer ponto. Aparece um alfinete, e a barra de baixo mostra latitude e longitude (ex.: -22.94642, -43.20000) em dois formatos. Passe ao resgate por telefone ou rádio, ou aperte Copiar.
  • Ruas detalhadas só existem nas regiões que foram baixadas. Fora delas, aparece o mapa-múndi.
  • Buscar um lugar: digite na caixa no canto superior esquerdo: cidade, bairro, montanha ou lago, no mundo todo para cidades e em detalhe nos países com mapa de ruas no kit. Digite hospital ou aeroporto para ver os mais próximos de onde o mapa está mostrando.
  • O botão ◎ minha localização só aparece no computador que está rodando o kit. Celulares conectados pelo Wi-Fi não conseguem usar o GPS aqui; para isso, use o Organic Maps no celular.
  • Marcadores compartilhados: toque num ponto e em 📍 Marcar para todos: escolha água, abrigo, ponto de encontro, perigo, atendimento médico, comida, energia ou outro, dê um nome e uma observação. Todos conectados ao kit (computador e celulares) veem os marcadores em segundos; toque num marcador para ver os detalhes ou remover. O compartilhamento precisa do kit rodando no Windows (o Windows pode pedir para liberar o "Windows PowerShell" no firewall: clique em Permitir); nos outros casos, os marcadores ficam só naquele aparelho.
  • O mapa precisa do programa de início rodando. Não funciona abrindo a página como arquivo.

Usar nos celulares

Celulares não rodam programas do pendrive. Por isso, o computador faz o trabalho e os celulares acessam como se fosse um site:

  1. Ligue o Roteador Wi-Fi / Hotspot do celular. Funciona sem sinal e sem dados.
  2. Conecte o computador a esse Wi-Fi. Ou ponha o computador e os celulares no mesmo roteador Wi-Fi.
  3. Inicie o kit no computador. A janela preta mostra endereços como http://192.168.43.20:8080/, e a página inicial mostra um código QR: aponte a câmera do celular para abrir o kit, sem digitar nada. Um celular já conectado mostra o mesmo código na página inicial, para passar ao próximo.
  4. Em cada celular, abra o navegador e digite esse endereço exatamente, incluindo :8080.

Vários celulares podem usar ao mesmo tempo. Se aparecerem vários endereços, tente o que começa com 192.168. No Android, dá para instalar antes os apps de apps/android/ (Kiwix, PocketPal AI, Organic Maps, Meshtastic) para usar a biblioteca, a IA e os mapas sem computador.

iPhone ou iPad? Veja a configuração para iPhone e iPad: roteador, apps, como copiar livros e modelos de IA do pendrive e salvar os cartões em PDF.

Problemas?

A janela preta diz que a IA não iniciouFeche e inicie de novo, escolhendo um modelo menor. Computadores antigos, com menos de 8 GB de memória, precisam do menor. Feche outros programas para liberar memória.
"IA: desligada" na página InícioO programa de início não está rodando ou foi fechado. Inicie de novo e mantenha a janela preta aberta.
Respostas muito lentasÉ normal em computadores antigos. Desmarque Consultar a biblioteca, faça perguntas mais curtas, ou reinicie com um modelo menor. Uma porta USB 3 (geralmente azul) carrega mais rápido.
O celular não abre o endereçoConfira se o celular e o computador estão no mesmo Wi-Fi ou roteador. Digite o endereço exatamente, com http:// e :8080. No Windows, permita o acesso quando o firewall perguntar (ou libere "llama-server" e "kiwix-serve" no Firewall do Windows Defender). Tente outro endereço da lista.
"Biblioteca: desligada"Logo depois de iniciar, a biblioteca leva alguns segundos para abrir os livros do pendrive: a página inicial muda para "ligada" sozinha (ou aperte F5). Se continuar desligada, o pendrive não tem arquivos da biblioteca ou o servidor não iniciou: reinicie o kit.
O mapa está vazio ou mostra uma mensagemAbra pelo programa de início (http://localhost:8080/map.html), não como arquivo. Se disser que não há mapas, o pendrive foi montado sem eles.
Mac: "não pode ser aberto"Botão direito em START-MAC.command → Abrir → Abrir. Ou no Terminal: bash /Volumes/<pendrive>/START-MAC.command.
Linux: permissão negadaRode com bash START-LINUX.sh (não com ./). O programa copia os arquivos para /tmp se o pendrive não deixar rodar.
Idioma erradoUse o menu de idioma no canto superior direito de qualquer página.
"Não deu para falar com o ajudante do kit"O kit foi atualizado enquanto rodava, ou a janela de início foi fechada. Desligue com uma tecla na janela preta e inicie de novo.
Os servidores continuam rodando depois de fechar o navegadorÉ de propósito: os celulares podem estar usando o kit. Quando terminar, use o botão vermelho Desligar o kit na página inicial.

Desligar e economizar bateria

  • Fechar o navegador NÃO desliga o kit: a IA, a biblioteca e os mapas continuam rodando (os celulares podem estar usando). Para desligar, clique no botão vermelho ⏻ Desligar o kit na página inicial (no computador que roda o kit), ou clique na janela preta e aperte qualquer tecla, ou feche essa janela (Windows). No Mac / Linux, aperte Ctrl+C na janela dele. Depois, remova o pendrive com segurança.
  • Ligue a ⚡ Economia na página inicial para a bateria durar mais.
  • Na bateria, a IA gasta muita energia enquanto escreve. Desligue o kit quando não estiver usando e diminua o brilho da tela.
  • Guarde uma cópia reserva do pendrive e os cartões impressos em outro lugar.

Iniciar um computador pelo pendrive (se o disco dele falhar)

Um kit tornado inicializável com o MAKE-BOOTABLE.bat (Windows) consegue iniciar quase qualquer PC, mesmo um cujo disco ou Windows não funciona mais, no Linux Mint, um sistema gratuito que roda do pendrive. Daí dá para usar o kit inteiro e copiar seus arquivos do disco do computador para um lugar seguro. Nada é instalado nem alterado no computador.

Tornar o pendrive inicializável (uma vez)

  1. Isso apaga o pendrive. Se ele já tem um kit, copie antes o kit para o computador com o COPY-KIT.bat → Este computador.
  2. Clique duas vezes no MAKE-BOOTABLE.bat, permita o acesso de administrador, digite a letra do pendrive e depois digite ERASE. Ele instala o Ventoy (gratuito, de código aberto) e baixa o Linux Mint XFCE (~2,9 GB), conferindo os dois com os checksums oficiais.
  3. Copie o kit de volta para o pendrive com o COPY-KIT.bat (ou monte com o MAKE-USB.bat). O kit funciona no Windows, Mac e Linux exatamente como antes. Rode o MAKE-BOOTABLE.bat de novo mais tarde para atualizar o Linux Mint; com o pendrive já inicializável, ele não apaga nada.

Iniciar um computador por ele

  1. Conecte o pendrive, ligue o computador e fique apertando a tecla do menu de boot: geralmente F12, F11, F9, F8 ou Esc (costuma aparecer rapidinho na tela). Escolha o pendrive.
  2. No menu do Ventoy escolha linuxmint… e depois Boot in normal mode. Na primeira vez num computador com Secure Boot, o Ventoy pede para registrar a chave: siga as telas uma vez.
  3. O Linux Mint inicia em um ou dois minutos. Abra o gerenciador de arquivos, abra o pendrive SURVIVAL, clique com o botão direito na pasta → Abrir no Terminal e digite bash START-LINUX.sh. O kit abre no Firefox.
  4. Salvar seus arquivos: o disco do computador aparece no gerenciador de arquivos (se ainda funcionar); copie o que importa para o pendrive ou outro disco.

Funciona na maioria dos PCs e nos Macs Intel (segure Option ao ligar). Computadores Apple com chip M1 ou mais novo não iniciam Linux por pendrive desse jeito. O que você muda dentro do Linux Mint (configurações, senhas de Wi-Fi) é esquecido ao desligar; os arquivos salvos no pendrive SURVIVAL ficam.

Compartilhar o kit (sem internet)

Dê uma cópia para a família, os vizinhos ou um abrigo. Num computador Windows, clique duas vezes em COPY-KIT.bat no pendrive.

  1. Copiar de: este kit já vem escolhido.
  2. Copiar para: o outro pendrive, ou Este computador para guardar uma cópia no computador (com atalho na área de trabalho).
  3. Confira a linha verde Cabe e clique em Copiar. Se o outro pendrive for FAT32, clique antes em Formatar em exFAT (isso apaga o pendrive).

Se a cópia parar, clique em Copiar de novo: os arquivos já copiados são pulados. Num Mac ou Linux, copie todos os arquivos e pastas de um pendrive para o outro pelo gerenciador de arquivos.

Atualizar o pendrive (precisa de internet)

Mais fácil: no computador Windows que está rodando o kit, abra Meu kit e clique em Adicionar, Procurar atualizações ou Verificar / reparar. Ou clique duas vezes em MAKE-USB.bat no pendrive, escolha o pendrive e clique em Procurar atualizações. Ele mostra edições novas da biblioteca, um motor de IA mais novo, mapas com mais de um ano e um Survival-Data mais novo, com os tamanhos, e atualiza se você confirmar. Na mesma janela dá para marcar mais livros, mapas ou idiomas e clicar em Montar o pendrive. Com internet, as páginas e ferramentas do kit vêm direto da versão mais nova no GitHub. O botão 🔄 Procurar atualizações da página inicial avisa quando sai uma versão nova. Se o kit estava rodando durante a atualização, desligue e inicie de novo para usar a versão nova. As instruções completas estão em LEIA-ME.md (Português) e README.md (English), no pendrive.
