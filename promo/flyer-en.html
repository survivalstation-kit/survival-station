Survival Station

A free emergency kit that works with no internet.

Survival Station puts first-aid know-how, maps, radio guidance and an AI assistant on a USB stick. Plug it into any laptop and it just runs: no internet, no install, no account.

Governments across Europe and the Americas advise every household to be ready for at least 72 hours on its own. Food, water and a torch are on every list. Reliable information, when the internet and phone networks are down, usually isn't.

  • 💬AI assistant, offlineAnswers in plain words and shows the library articles it used.
  • 📚Offline libraryMedical guides, water, food, shelter, repairs and Wikipedia.
  • 🗺️Maps with searchFind towns and the nearest hospital; read coordinates to rescuers.
  • ⛑️Emergency cardsCPR, bleeding, burns, safe water, signals. Printable.
  • 📡Radio guideEmergency frequencies and how to set up a handheld radio.
  • 🛠️ToolsCPR rhythm, bleach drops for water, SOS flash and whistle.
  • 📱Phones tooPhones on the laptop's hotspot use it all in the browser.
  • 🌎English and PortuguêsEverything switches language, including the AI's answers.

Get it free

Scan the code, or type the address. Build the stick on a Windows computer with internet (32-256 GB USB). It then runs on Windows, Mac and Linux.

Free and open source (MIT licence). Share it: copying a finished kit to another stick needs no internet.

github.com/survivalstation-kit/survival-station

Survival Station is a reference tool, not a substitute for emergency services, doctors or official instructions. The AI can be wrong. Call for help when you can, follow your authorities, and use radios only as the law allows. Official preparedness advice: ready.gov/kit (USA), getprepared.gc.ca (Canada), prepare.campaign.gov.uk (UK), redcross.org.
