# Video script (about 2½ minutes)

For YouTube, or as shorter clips for TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. Record the screen with the Windows Game Bar
(**Win+G**) or OBS Studio, and the phone with its own screen recorder. Use a neutral, well-known city on the map,
not your own area, and keep personal files, addresses and notifications off screen.

**Before recording:** start the kit, open the home page, set the browser zoom to 125% so text is readable, and
have a phone on the laptop's hotspot.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | A USB stick in a hand, then plugged into a laptop. | "When the power or the internet goes down, most of us lose access to everything we'd normally look up. This fixes that." |
| 0:10 | Title: **Survival Station: free, offline, open source**. | "Survival Station is a free emergency kit that runs from a USB stick. No internet, nothing to install." |
| 0:20 | Double-click START-WINDOWS.bat; the home page opens. | "Plug it in, double-click one file, and everything opens in the browser." |
| 0:30 | Open **Emergency**, scroll the cards (CPR, bleeding, water). | "Emergency cards first: short, checked steps for the first minutes. They work even if nothing else does, and they print." |
| 0:45 | **Ask AI**: "How do I purify water with household bleach?" Show the answer and the sources, with "Emergency card" first. | "A local AI answers in plain words. It looks up the kit's own cards and an offline medical library first, and shows its sources. It can still be wrong, so check anything medical." |
| 1:10 | **Map**: type "hospital" over a known city; click one. | "Offline maps find towns and the nearest hospitals, and give coordinates to read out to rescuers." |
| 1:25 | **Tools**: start the CPR rhythm (with sound), then the water calculator. | "Tools that need no AI: a CPR rhythm, bleach drops for any amount of water, an SOS flash and whistle." |
| 1:40 | **Radio** page, scrolling the frequency table. | "A radio page with emergency frequencies and how to set up a handheld radio." |
| 1:50 | Phone: scan the QR code on the home page; the kit opens on the phone. | "Phones on the laptop's hotspot use everything in their browser. Scan the code, done." |
| 2:05 | **COPY-KIT** window with the time estimate. | "And one stick can make another without internet, so one person can equip a whole street." |
| 2:15 | GitHub page; text: **github.com/survivalstation-kit/survival-station**. | "It's free and open source, in English and Portuguese. Build yours now, while you still have internet. The link is below." |
| 2:30 | End card with the link and "Not a substitute for emergency services." | (no voice) |

**Description text:**

> Survival Station is a free, open-source emergency kit on a USB stick: offline first aid, maps with place
> search, a radio guide, tools and a local AI assistant that cites its sources. No internet or install needed;
> phones join over the laptop's hotspot. Download: https://github.com/survivalstation-kit/survival-station
> It is a reference tool, not a substitute for emergency services, doctors or official instructions.
